What we are working on this season, so you know what is coming.

**Three books in edit.** A short book on money for people who find money boring, a book on keeping a house without it keeping you, and a second book from Ada Finch on writing for the screen. All under a hundred pages, as the rule says.

**One recording.** Tomas Reyes is reading *Working Hours* in full. It will sit beside the text in the reader, and members will hear it first.

**The Field Guide, printed.** Our first book goes to paper this autumn: a paperback with the companion bound in, and the PDF and EPUB with every copy. Members will see it in the store first.

If you want to hear about each of these the day it lands, the newsletter is one line in your account settings.
