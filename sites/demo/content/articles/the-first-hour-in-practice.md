When *Working Hours* came out I asked readers to send me one line: what does your first hour look like? Sixty replied. Here are six, lightly edited, with permission.

**A nurse, on shifts.** "My first hour is at 3pm on days I work nights. It's the same hour; it just moves. I write the one line on the back of my hand."

**A parent of twins.** "There is no first hour until the twins are at nursery, so my first hour is at 9:40. I used to count that as failure. Now I count it as 9:40."

**A freelance illustrator.** "One drawing, no email, no phone in the room. I finish more in that hour than in the four after it, and I've stopped pretending otherwise."

**A civil servant.** "I plan it the night before in the notes app, one line. If the line is more than one line I have not planned it, I have listed it."

**A student.** "Library, 8am, the one chapter. The trick was choosing a dull seat. Exciting seats have windows."

**A retired teacher.** "I thought this was for working people. It is for anyone with a day. My first hour is the garden."

Four days in five is a working life. None of them keep it every day. All of them keep coming back.
