For a season we are recording one chapter from each book, read by its author, and giving it away. This is chapter three of *The Quiet Room*, which is the chapter people write to us about.

Its argument is that ten minutes with nothing in them is not rest and not a practice — it is the only part of the day when you can hear what you actually think, because it is the only part when nothing is talking to you. Maren is careful to claim very little: not calm, not clarity, only that the claims on your attention land differently for an hour afterwards.

It runs eleven minutes. The rest of the book is in the library, and the first chapter is free.
