Every manuscript that comes through Northlight is read aloud before it is accepted, and again before it is printed. Not skimmed, not proofread — read, at speaking pace, to an empty room, by someone who is not the author.

The ear catches what the eye forgives. The sentence that cannot be said in one breath. The word that looked impressive on the page and cannot be said to a person. The paragraph where the tone changes, which you hear in your own voice before you know why.

## The rule for everyone else

You do not need a press to do this. Before you send anything that matters — the email that asks for something, the page that explains what the thing is — read it aloud once. Fix only what you stumbled on. When you can read it through, send it.

That is the whole of chapter four of *Plain Words*, and it is the chapter people write to us about most.
