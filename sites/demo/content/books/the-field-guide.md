# Starting small

The first day is the easiest and the least important. Choose something so small that skipping it would be embarrassing: one page, one push-up, one line in a notebook. ==The size of the practice is not the point; the daily return to it is.==

Most plans fail on the third day, when the novelty is gone and the result is not yet visible. Plan for the third day. Put the notebook where you will trip over it.

# Keeping count

A count is a kindness to your future self. It turns a vague feeling of having been good into a number you can look at. ==Count the days, never the results: the results come on their own schedule, and the days are yours.==

When the chain breaks, and it will, do not start the count at zero. Start it at one. A chain of one is a chain.

# What it becomes

After a season the practice stops being a decision. You do it the way you brush your teeth: without a vote. This is the whole reward, and it is larger than it sounds. ==A life is mostly made of what you do without deciding to.=={sun}
