**Companion** — the summary, insights and quiz that come with every chapter of a book.

**Member** — a reader with a live membership: every book opens, and the chat with a coach.

**Patron** — a member with a private hour each month and the fastest chat.

**Session** — an hour with a coach, booked in the app, by video.

**Trophy** — what a chapter becomes when you pass its quiz: bronze for the first, silver at halfway, gold for the whole book.

**Highlight** — a line the editors marked in the text, drawn as a wavy underline; your own highlights are washes of colour.
