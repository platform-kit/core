A session is an hour with a coach by video, booked from the Coaching tab of your account.

## Booking

Pick a day and a time from what is open. Members' calls and Patron hours are included in the plan; a single session on its own is $60, paid as it is booked.

## What to bring

One chapter and one real situation. The best sessions are small: a habit you want to keep, a piece of writing that will not come right, a week that got away from you.

## Afterwards

The coach writes first — a line in your chat within a few minutes of the call ending — and the conversation carries on there for a week.
