There are two plans, Member and Patron, and a free tier underneath them both.

## Reader (free)

The first chapters of every book, every article, the wiki and the guides, and the support chat. No account is needed to read; an account keeps your place, your highlights and your trophies.

## Member

Every chapter of every book, the chat with a coach (answered within 48 hours), and the monthly members' call.

## Patron

Everything in Member, a private hour with a coach each month, chat answered within 24 hours, and each new book the day it lands.

## Leaving

Cancel from your account; the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Any book you bought outright stays yours, as do your highlights and trophies.
