Every book carries the editors' marks: the lines worth noticing in each chapter, drawn as a wavy underline. They are suggestions, not instructions.

Your own highlights are yours: select a passage and mark it, and it is kept on your account and shown on every device you sign in to. A highlight in a chapter you have not yet opened as a member waits for you.

The marks and your highlights are both gathered at the back of a printed copy, with their page numbers.
