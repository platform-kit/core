Northlight Press is a small independent press founded in 2024. It publishes short, practical books — on attention, craft and rest — and edits each to what a busy reader will finish.

## What we publish

A book has to have one idea a reader can use the same week, and it has to fit in an evening. Most of ours are under eighty pages. Anything longer goes back to its author with a note that says so.

## What we do not publish

Books that need a second book to understand. Books whose idea is in the title. Anything we would not read aloud.

## How to submit

Send the first chapter and a paragraph on what the book asks the reader to do. If we want the rest we will ask within a fortnight.
