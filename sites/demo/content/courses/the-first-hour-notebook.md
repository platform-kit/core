Three lessons that come with the notebook: how to set it up, an hour a morning in practice, and what to look back on after a month.

## setting-up

Number the pages first. Leave the first two blank: they become the index once you know what the notebook is for.

## a-month-in

Read the month back in one sitting. Mark the three mornings that changed something, and write on the index page what they had in common.
