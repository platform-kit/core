Four lessons, all open at once, on keeping one hour of the day for nothing in particular. A chapter of *The Quiet Room*, an article, and a week to try it.

## why-quiet

An hour is long enough to get bored, and boredom is where the quiet starts. Less than that and the day never quite lets go.

## a-week-of-it

Keep the same hour for seven days. Write one line at the end of each: what you noticed when there was nothing to do.
