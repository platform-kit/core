Six lessons, two days apart, on saying what you mean in as few words as it takes. Read a chapter of *Plain Words*, try it on a page of your own, and finish with a short check.

## welcome

Most writing that is hard to read is hard to read on purpose: long words feel safer than short ones. This course is about the opposite habit.

Watch the short film, then go on to the first chapter when it opens.

## one-page

Take one page you wrote this month. Cross out every word that does not change the meaning, then read what is left aloud.

The worksheet has the three questions to ask of each sentence.
