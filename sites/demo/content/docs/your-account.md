Sign in with an email address and a password; if there is no account for the address, one is made. A confirmation link goes to the address, and a few things — your results, the chat — wait until it has been opened.

Your account keeps your place in every book, your highlights, your trophies, your orders and your membership. Sign in on another device and all of it is there.

To change your password, use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in form. To leave, write to the support chat and we will close the account and remove what it holds.
