The reader is built for a phone first. The chapter bar at the foot turns pages; the switch above it shows the summary in place of the text; where a chapter has a recording, the player sits above the bar.

Highlights: press and hold a passage. The quiz: at the end of every chapter, or from the chapter bar. Your place is kept the moment you turn a page.

The in-browser voice — a chapter spoken on your own machine when no recording exists — is offered on a desktop only; on a phone the recordings the press has made are what plays.
