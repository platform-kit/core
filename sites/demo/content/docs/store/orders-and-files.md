A book bought outright opens in the reader for good. A printed copy comes with the PDF and the EPUB, which appear under "Your files" on the book's store page and on its cover page in the reader, for an hour at a time, as many times as you like.

Printed copies are made to order and ship within a week; the tracking number arrives by email when the parcel leaves. A printed copy or a file can be bought without an account; make one later with the same address and the order is there.

All sales are final; a damaged copy is replaced without charge — write to the support chat.
