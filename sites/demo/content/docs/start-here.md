1. Open the library and read the first chapters of any book — no account needed.
2. Make an account to keep your place, your highlights and your quiz results.
3. Join as a member to open every chapter and talk to a coach.

The first book most people finish is *The Field Guide*: three chapters, about twenty minutes.
