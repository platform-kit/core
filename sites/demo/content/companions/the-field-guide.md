# Starting small

## Summary

Begin with a practice so small that skipping it would be embarrassing — a page, a push-up, a line. The size is not the point; the daily return is. Plans fail on the third day, when novelty is gone and results are not yet visible, so plan for the third day and put the tools where you will trip over them.

## Essence

- Choose a practice too small to skip.
- The daily return matters more than the size.
- Plan for the third day, not the first.

# Keeping count

## Summary

A count turns a vague sense of having been good into a number you can look at. Count the days, never the results, which come on their own schedule. When the chain breaks, start the count at one, not zero: a chain of one is a chain.

# What it becomes

## Summary

After a season the practice stops being a decision and happens without a vote, like brushing your teeth. That is the whole reward, and it is larger than it sounds: a life is mostly made of what you do without deciding to.
