# One thing at a time

## Summary

The tiredness of modern work comes from holding many things open at once. The cure is arrangement, not concentration: one thing in front of you, the rest out of reach, and each thing put away before the next is taken up. Finished work stops costing you; open work costs you every minute.

## Essence

- Put the rest out of reach, not out of mind.
- Put each thing away before the next.
- Open work costs you every minute it stays open.

# The list that ends

## Summary

A list that grows without end is a weather system. Write the day's list — five or six things — and no further; what is left off is waiting, not lost. When the last item is crossed out the day's work is done, and anything more is a gift to tomorrow. The long list stays, looked at weekly, holding what you need not.

## Essence

- A useful list has an end.
- Five or six things; the rest are waiting.
- The short list is for doing, the long one for forgetting safely.
