## Selling your time here

These terms are between you and Northlight Press. They are short on purpose, and you should read
them.

**What you are offering.** You set what you offer, how long it takes and what it costs. You decide
your own hours. Nobody here books you outside them, and nobody here changes your prices.

**What we do.** We put what you offer on the site, take the payment, and pay you what the rules you
were shown say you are owed. What you have earned is written down as it is earned, and you can see
every line of it.

**When you are paid.** Money is held for thirty days after it is collected, so that a refund in that
window reverses a line rather than becoming a debt. After that it is payable, and it is paid to the
account you connected.

**Stopping.** You can retire anything you offer at any time. Sessions already booked still happen.
