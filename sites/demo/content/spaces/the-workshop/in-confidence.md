A sealed room. The messages in here are encrypted on the devices that write them and decrypted on
the devices that read them, so the site holds a ciphertext and nothing else — and neither search,
nor a bot, nor the staff reading a report can look inside. A copy of what was said cannot be
handed over by anybody who does not already have it.
