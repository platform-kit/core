The first frost arrives without announcing itself. One morning the grass is stiff and the light
has changed, and the year has turned over while nobody was reading the signs.

## What the cold is for

A winter is not an absence of growing. It is the part of the cycle where the work happens out of
sight, in the root rather than the leaf, and the plant that skips it is the plant that does not
come back.

## Keeping the work

Everything worth having through a long season is put by in a season that was not long. That is the
whole of it, and the reason it is hard is that the putting by has to happen while the weather is
still good.
