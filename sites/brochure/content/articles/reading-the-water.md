A river tells you where its stones are, if you will read the surface instead of looking through it.

## The seam

Where two currents meet there is a line of quiet water, and everything the river is carrying
gathers along it. Find the seam and you have found the day's work.

## Patience as a technique

The people who catch nothing are almost never the people who cannot cast. They are the people who
could not stand to wait.
