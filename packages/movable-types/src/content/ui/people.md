## role

### provider

# THE WORD THIS SITE USES for the person who delivers what it sells, said once and nowhere else.
# The kit calls the ROLE provider because the kit does not know what the business is; a site says
# Coach, Healer, Therapist, Consultant, Tutor. Every surface reads these fields and no component
# ever writes one of these words itself.
#
# A person may also set their OWN title on their profile, and wherever a surface names THAT
# person it wins over every field here — so a practice of five can have four Therapists and one
# Supervisor without the site changing its word. The class is the site's; the person is their own.
name: Provider
plural: Providers
# the same word inside a sentence: "an hour with a provider"
a: a provider
# the possessive somebody sees about their own: "message your provider"
your: your provider
# what the WORK is called, where a surface names the activity rather than the person
work: Sessions
# one occasion of it
session: session
