# PlatformKit and its alternatives

*A capability-by-capability comparison of PlatformKit with the products and services whose core jobs it can cover. This is not a claim of feature-for-feature parity: the final column names what PlatformKit deliberately leaves to each comparable. The specifications, especially `kit-principles`, are authoritative.*

---

The table maps the comparable's central job to the PlatformKit module that covers it. PlatformKit's overall objectives and operating model are described in [PURPOSE.md](./PURPOSE.md); the detailed capabilities are listed in [FEATURES.md](./FEATURES.md).

## What it replaces — and what it leaves to each, on purpose

Each module carries one comparable's core job, and documents what it deliberately does not do that the comparable does.

Community and social networking are related but separate: one row describes spaces for a site's members, and another describes the site's own network, which can connect to Bluesky when the site and each member opt in.
