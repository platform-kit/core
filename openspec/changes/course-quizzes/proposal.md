# Proposal

## Why

A quiz in a course did not work like a quiz in a course. The lesson showed a button that sent the reader to the form's own page. When they finished, the form told them to go back and mark the lesson complete themselves, and the course never showed their score. Kajabi, Teachable and Thinkific all run a quiz inside its lesson, record the score, complete the lesson when the quiz is passed, and show staff each student's results.

## What Changes

- **The quiz runs in the lesson.** A form lesson shows the form inside the player, under the lesson's own text, not as a link to another page.
- **Passing completes the lesson.** When a reader finishes a form, and passes it where it is scored, the lesson is recorded done by the server at that moment. An unscored form completes its lesson when it is finished. Nobody marks a quiz lesson done by hand.
- **The result is shown in the player.** A pass shows the score and the way on to the next lesson. A fail shows the score, the pass mark and **Try again**. A quiz already passed shows the reader's best score and lets them retake it; a retake never undoes the completion.
- **The scores are kept and shown.** Every attempt is already kept with its score. The course shows the reader their best score on each quiz in the curriculum, and administrators see each person's quiz scores in the course's enrollments.

## Non-goals

- Grading free-text answers, timed quizzes, or limiting the number of attempts.
- Changing how a form is written, scored or graded.
