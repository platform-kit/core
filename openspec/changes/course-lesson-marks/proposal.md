# Proposal

## Why

The e-book marks the lines a reader should notice with a wavy coloured underline, `==text=={colour}`, the colour saying what the line speaks to. A course lesson could not: its text showed the markup as typed. A course is where the lines a student must grasp matter most, and Kajabi-style lessons rely on the author drawing the eye to them.

## What Changes

- A lesson's text, and a course's description, draw the e-book's marks the way the e-book does, in its five colours.
- The marks are one shared piece of the theme, used by the e-book and by courses, rather than code of the reader's own.

## Non-goals

- A student's own highlights in a lesson.
- Marks anywhere else that renders markdown.
