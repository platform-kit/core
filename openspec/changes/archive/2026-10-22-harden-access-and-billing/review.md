# Review

Written 2026-10-19, before any fix landed. Three read-only reviews were run over `packages/kit/supabase` (schemas and Edge Functions) and the browser code: ownership, prices/roles/public doors, and webhooks/links/secrets. The four most serious findings were then reproduced against the local demo stack as a signed-out visitor and as `member2@example.com`.

Paths are relative to `packages/kit/`. "Fix here" means this change fixes it, together with the test that would have caught it. "Proposed separately" means it is outside this change's guarantees or needs a product decision; it is listed so it isn't lost.

## How the surfaces were found

- **Records:** every table with a customer's data and its RLS policies. Every `SECURITY DEFINER` function, and whether anon and authenticated are revoked by name; Supabase's default privileges leave a function open otherwise (`schemas/10-foundation.sql`, the note above `public_profile`). Every Edge Function that takes an id from the request.
- **Prices:** every field `create-checkout-session` reads from the body, and every other path that charges.
- **Roles and entitlements:** every writer of `profiles.roles`/`role`, `entitlements`, `purchases`, `subscriptions`, the space, team and channel membership tables, and `provider_offerings`.
- **Webhooks:** every function with `verify_jwt = false` in `supabase/config.toml.template`.
- **Links:** every `createSignedUrl` call, its lifetime and its check.
- **Doors:** every function or RPC a signed-out visitor can reach that writes.
- **Secrets:** the Vite `envPrefix`, every `import.meta.env` read, and the built output.

### Links

Customer links (purchased files, recordings, support attachments, form files) are signed for one hour after a check of the requester. Printer links are signed for seven days, sent only to the printer and never stored or shown to a customer; the printer fetches files when the job is created and may retry over days, so seven days stays. O8 is the only link finding.
