## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every community place is drawn to one measure

The community page SHALL define the page width and padding, the card ground, edge, corner and shadow, the gap between cards, and the title face and size, and Home, the Courses place and the sessions SHALL draw with them; a site's own place MAY read them. A page outside the community SHALL keep its own look.

#### Scenario: moving between places

- **WHEN** a member opens Home, the course, the sessions and the diagnostics in turn
- **THEN** each starts at the same left edge and height, with the same title face and size and the same card ground and corner

Verified by: the five places measured and screenshotted in a browser at 1290 (light and dark) and 390
